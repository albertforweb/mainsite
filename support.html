Support

Need help signing in or managing your Adventure account? Here are the most common questions and how to reach us.

I forgot my password

Contact us and we will reset it for you after verifying your identity. Passwords cannot be recovered because they are stored only as secure hashes.

I can't sign in

  • Double-check your username and password for typos.
  • Make sure Caps Lock is off.
  • Confirm your account status is active.

How do I update my profile?

After signing in, your account details can be managed by the site administrators. Email us to request any updates.

Contact us
