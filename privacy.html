Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

1. What we collect

Adventure collects only the information you provide directly: your username, display name, email address and password. We never sell or share your personal data with third parties.

2. How we use your data

  • To authenticate you when you sign in.
  • To display your profile and avatar.
  • To keep your account secure and recoverable.

3. Passwords & security

Your password is never stored in plain text. It is salted and hashed using a strong one-way cryptographic function before it is saved.

4. Cookies

Adventure does not use tracking cookies. Your browser and network may store essential cookies required to keep you signed in.

5. Your rights

You may request a copy, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting Support.
